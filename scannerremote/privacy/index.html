Scanner Remote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Scanner Remote is an iPhone app from ElectricalFlo LLC. It connects to your radio scanner over Bluetooth, lets you control it and listen to it, and keeps a list of the calls it heard while the app was open. This page explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing. Everything stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party tracking tools. The app does not use the internet, so it has nowhere to send information, and ElectricalFlo LLC never receives your data.

What the app saves on your phone

WhatWhy
Recordings of calls heard while the app is openSo you can play them back in History
For each call: the time, length, system, department, channel, talkgroup, unit ID and frequency, as reported by your scannerTo show and search the History list and the activity statistics
Names you give to unit IDs, and your watch listTo show names and flag the calls you care about
Your settings, and the name of the scanner you connected toTo remember your choices and reconnect automatically

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it (see below).

You are in control

  • How long calls are kept: in Settings you choose 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days. Older calls and their audio are deleted automatically. Calls you star are kept until you delete them.
  • Delete a call: swipe it in History.
  • Delete everything: delete the app. That removes its saved calls, names and settings from your phone.

Sharing

If you choose to share a recording, or export your unit names, iOS opens its standard share sheet and the file goes wherever you send it. The app does not share anything on its own.

Bluetooth permission

The app asks to use Bluetooth only to find your scanner, control it and play its audio. It does not use Bluetooth for any other purpose.

Backups

The audio recordings are marked so they are left out of iPhone and iCloud backups. The History list and your unit names are ordinary app data, so they may be included in a backup that you make of your phone.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Radio laws

Rules about listening to, recording and sharing radio traffic differ by country, state and city. You are responsible for following the laws where you are. Encrypted transmissions can't be heard.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
