Scanner Remote — Support

Control your scanner and hear it on your iPhone.

Scanner Remote connects to your radio scanner over Bluetooth. You can hold channels, change the volume and squelch, open the scanner's menu, skip a channel, listen to the audio on your phone, and look back at the calls you heard. It was built and tested with the SDS150.

Getting started

  1. Turn Bluetooth on at the scanner.
  2. If you use the scanner maker's own phone app, close it. The scanner talks to one app at a time.
  3. Open Scanner Remote and tap Continue. Allow Bluetooth when your phone asks.
  4. Tap Look for the scanner, then tap your scanner in the list.
  5. Tap Listen to hear it. Next time you open the app, it reconnects by itself.

Common questions

The app can't find my scanner
  • Check that Bluetooth is on at the scanner and on your phone.
  • Close the scanner maker's own app, and any other app that connects to the scanner.
  • Move the phone closer, and try Look for the scanner again.
  • Check that Scanner Remote is allowed to use Bluetooth: Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Bluetooth.
It connects, then drops

The scanner sometimes drops the Bluetooth link. The app reconnects by itself within a few seconds. If it keeps dropping, check the scanner's battery and move closer.

I can't hear anything
  • Tap Listen. Audio plays only while Listen is on. (You can switch on "Listen when the scanner connects" in Settings.)
  • Check the phone's volume and silent switch, and the scanner's own volume and squelch.
  • Encrypted transmissions can't be heard on a scanner.
History is empty, or a call is missing

Calls are recorded only while the app is open and connected to the scanner. Calls heard while the app was closed or the phone was locked are not saved. Calls older than the time you chose in Settings are deleted, unless you starred them.

How do I keep calls for longer?

Open Settings and change Keep calls for (1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days). Star a call to keep it permanently.

How do I name a radio?

In History, swipe a call to the right and tap Name unit. You can also manage names, and import or export them as a CSV file, in Settings under Unit names.

What does AVOID do?

It sends your scanner's own AVOID key. The scanner avoids the channel it is on, and that channel stops coming up. To bring a channel back, use your scanner's own controls.

How do I delete my information?

Swipe a call in History to delete it, or delete the app to remove everything it saved. See the privacy policy.
